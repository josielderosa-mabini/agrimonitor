System Information

AgriMonitor is an IoT-based irrigation water level and flow monitoring system developed for small-scale farms. It uses sensor technology and an ESP32 microcontroller to monitor irrigation water conditions and is designed to provide real-time information and alerts through a web-based dashboard.

SYSTEM NAME AgriMonitor

IoT-based irrigation water level and flow monitoring system.

MICROCONTROLLER ESP32

Processes sensor readings and supports Wi-Fi communication.

WATER LEVEL SENSOR Analog Water Level Sensor

Detects changes in water level within the monitored water container.

WATER FLOW SENSOR YF-S201

Measures the rate of water flowing through the irrigation pipeline.

CONNECTIVITY Wi-Fi

Enables communication between the ESP32 and the monitoring platform.

MONITORING PLATFORM Web-Based Dashboard

Displays water level, flow rate, monitoring history, and system alerts.

SYSTEM STATUS Prototype Development

Hardware sensors have been tested. Online sensor data integration is currently being developed.
